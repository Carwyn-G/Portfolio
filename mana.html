Mana E-Bikes

This project was all about finding a way to appeal to a new market for this e-bike brand. The goal was an emphasis on simplicity and good vibes in a versatile digital format that would allow the content to reach its audience across a range of platforms.

Method

The artwork was first created in illustrator, and then animated in AfterEffects using a combination of rotoscoping and keyframing. The animation was then edited together with the product footage to create the final product. This project was created without the use of any AI tools.

Logo and Colors

The Mana e-bike logo is a simple wordmark in a heavily stylized font that provides a light fantasy feel, that can be taken even further in its animated form seen at the end of the video. The brand uses primarily white or black for its logo, but favors friendly, calming colors with connections to nature in broader applications.

Final Thoughts

Because the main illustrations were made as vector art, they're highly scaleable and can be adapted well to different screen sizes and layouts. The animation was also designed to loop seamlessly to provide additional flexibility in editing.
