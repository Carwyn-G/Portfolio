Athena Umbrella Co

This project called for a different style than many of my other works, which I took as an opportunity to flex other skills besides illustration. The goal was a campaign that would be simple, yet eye-catching. I also wanted to create branding that would make Athena Umbrella Co feel high quality while still being approachable to a wide market audience.

Method

This project was created almost entirely in photoshop. Only the logo was made in Illustrator. To keep this project within tight time constraints, stock images were used and edited rather than having a custom photoshoot. All designs were crafted exclusively with human skill, without any use of generative AI tools.

Logo and Colors

The logo uses a simple wordmark in a polished serif font. A slight modification was made to the T to give it a stronger curve on the underside of the horizontal bar that would be subtly remniscent of an umbrella. The primary brand color is a vivid, sunshine yellow chosen for its warmth and cheerfulness.

Final Thoughts

Extra attention to detail was given when designing the campaign images to line up the angle of the rain and corresponding protected area to match the angle of the A in the logo. This attention to detail provides a greater sense of cohesion that gives the final image a more polished feel.
