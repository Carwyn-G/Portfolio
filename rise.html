Rise Chocolate

When designing branding and packaging for this brand focused on chocolate bars with creative flavor combinations, I knew I wanted to make their labels really bold to match. I also wanted the design to be highly modular, so that labels could be made not just for limited, pre-determined flavors, but could easily be mixed and matched to still have clear packaging for any future flavor combinations as well.

Method

After some early pencil and paper sketches to decide the final idea I wanted to go with, the wrappers were created in Adobe Illustrator. Some stock vectors were used as a starting point for certain flavors, while others were illustrated from scratch by me. No AI was used at any stage of this design.

Logo and Colors

The logo and colors work together to embody a sunrise vibe, leaning into the brand's bright attitude and values of using sustainable cacao to ensure delicious chocolate for a long future to come.

Final Thoughts

As this design was for a Canadian brand, I needed to be mindful of accomodating the design to both the bilingual labelling requirements and general food labelling requirements. Also, given my goal of having a very modular design, I had to pair colors carefully to have good options for visual contrast no matter what flavor was being designed for. Having a spectrum of blues, oranges, and yellows as the brand colors helped ensure there was always an option with sufficient contrast.
