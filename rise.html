Rise Chocolate

When designing for this chocolate company focused on chocolate bars with creative flavor combinations, I knew I wanted to make their labels really bold to match. I also wanted the design to be highly modular, so that labels could be made not just for a limited set number of combinations, but were made up of components that could easily be mixed and matched to still have clear packaging for any future flavor combinations as well.
