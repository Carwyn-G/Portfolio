Welcome to my Portfolio Home!

As someone that has moved a lot and lived in several different places, the inspiration for my portfolio comes from all the many homes I've lived in. This house is not just one building, but a combination of homes I've lived in across three different countries.

Just like with my design projects, each move brought with it something entirely new, but in each new home, I always still made it my own, and made the best of whatever new challenges that location brought.
