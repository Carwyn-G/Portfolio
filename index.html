Rise Chocolate

The Brief

Designing branding and packaging for a Canadian-based chocolate company with an emphasis on ethically sourced cacao used in creative flavor combinations. Their use of bold flavors called for equally bold branding and packaging, but that could be readily adapted to any new flavor combination.

About the Design

The name lent itself to both the sunrise-inspired color scheme and the radiant layout. The radiant layout was also chosen for being easily modular, so that all available flavors can be freely mixed and matched, and new flavors can be easily introduced to the system.

The tossed pattern of images makes for a vibrant, exciting wrapper that catches the eye on shelves, while remaining clear and compliant with Canadian bilingual labelling requirements.

Athena Umbrella Company

The Brief

Designing clear branding and developing an advertising campaign for an umbrella company looking to stand out.

About the Design

My goal for the campaign and logo was something simple, but still bright. The primary brand color, rather than calling on themes of gloomy weather, is a vibrant sunshine yellow to invoke a feeling of clear skies even on the rainy days one would need an umbrella for.

The campaign takes that idea even further, showing the umbrellas providing a perfect haven of colorful protection from the grey and rain. These simple ads were designed for physical placement in outdoor spaces where they could be encountered on rainy days when they would be most relevant.

Mana E-Bikes

The Brief

Designing branding and developing a video ad campaign for an electric bike company wanting to appeal to people who aren't cyclists, seeking to emphasize the ease and effortlessness of their e-bikes.

About the Design

Just as the tagline says, the goal of this campaign was to make these e-bikes feel magical, to really show riding them as being akin to entering into a whole new world, one that feels peaceful and beautiful.

Greater Victoria Zoo

The Brief

To create a strong visual identity for a zoo near Victoria, BC, Canada, that would appeal to young families of both tourists and locals alike.

About the Design

To stand out from other area attractions with more reserved aesthetics, I wanted to use a lot of bright colors and modern-feeling illustrations, while still keeping some of the heart of the pacific northwest aesthetic that is so important to the area.

For this project I designed several animal illustrations for use on the zoo's map and signage, as well as a series of social media campaign images designed to promote the zoo as a fun and educational experience.

Author Kai Evan Sterling

The Brief

Designing a cohesive visual identity for the website and book covers of Kai Evan Sterling, a new author of an urban fantasy novel series.

About the Design

This project was all about creating book covers and an author website that would draw readers in to want to explore more. Sterling's novels are richly layered with worldbuilding elements, and so I chose the layered papercut aesthetic to give that impression of there always being another layer to explore.

In particular the goal of the website was to not just be home to some links to purchase the books, but a place readers could access bonus content and character art to deepen their appreciation for the books.

This project is still a living project with updates to come as the stories are finished and publication is finalized.
