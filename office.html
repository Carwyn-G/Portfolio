Kai Evan Sterling

This was a passion project for me, as I have been close with the author for many years. While they have not yet officially published, and the site uses placeholders for future links to buy the books, they wanted to start building a website and book covers that would be ready to launch as they near the conclusion of their series and aim for next steps.

It was a particularly fun project for me to explore the fictional world they've developed and come up with ways to bring it off the page in a way that would inspire curiosity without giving too much away.

Method

The design process started with the cover designs, first as pencil sketches and then as Illustrator artwork. InDesign was used in later stages to ensure proper alignment of text content on the spine and back cover. The website was built off the foundation the cover designs provided, taking the layered illustration and bringing it to life with subtle CSS animations and Javascript. Everything from the designs to the site code was my own work, without any AI use.

Logo and Colors

The logo used for Sterling's website is deliberately kept simple, cleanly presenting their name in a modern aesthetic that fits their work and the rest of their branding. The teal and berry colors were taken directly from important thematic and worldbuilding elements in the stories, while additional accent colors were chosen by me to complement them and bring greater depth to the designs..

Final Thoughts

Being able to work with a series of multiple books all at once rather than having to design each cover separately meant that I was able to use the big picture to consider how the covers could work with each other rather than just being separate but cohesive designs. This led to designs that strategically wrapped around the spine so that when the series sits on a shelf, the artwork forms together into a larger connected piece.
